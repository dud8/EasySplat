# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants in the EasySplat community are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive,
- give direct technical feedback without making it personal,
- focus criticism on code and behavior, not people,
- avoid harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks,
- help keep the project welcoming to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or intimidation,
- hate speech or discriminatory language,
- sustained disruption of project discussions,
- doxxing or sharing private information without permission,
- bad-faith behavior intended to exhaust, demean, or exclude other contributors.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
